I joined Bethesda.net as an engineer on the web team, where I helped migrate [the public website](https://bethesda.net) from a static content system to a React application backed by a headless CMS. I later became the lead for a squad focused on marketing and community initiatives, site performance, and public content discoverability.

Ahead of a major game launch, I moved to the Platform Reliability Team (<abbr>PRT</abbr>). There, I built an ephemeral load-testing platform running on AWS Elastic Container Service (<abbr>ECS</abbr>) that let engineers schedule isolated tests through Slack and automatically receive reports when they completed.

Teams used the platform to identify several performance bottlenecks before launch. We then addressed those issues to improve the platform’s readiness for launch-scale traffic.
