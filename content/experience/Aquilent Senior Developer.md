At Aquilent, I worked as a full-stack developer on federal web applications across health, public service, research, and enterprise systems. The projects included [HealthCare.gov](https://healthcare.gov), Veterans Affairs (<abbr>VA</abbr>) applications connected to [My HealtheVet](https://www.va.gov/health-care/manage-health/) and [VistA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VistA), and National Library of Medicine (<abbr>NLM</abbr>) systems supporting access to research and emergency preparedness resources.

In my final role at Aquilent, I led a team of four engineers responsible for the [HealthCare.gov Find Local Help](https://www.healthcare.gov/find-local-help/) application. It was my first formal team leadership role and my first opportunity to lead the design and integration of REST APIs and React front ends. That work established a foundation for the web platforms, services, and engineering leadership that became the focus of my career.
