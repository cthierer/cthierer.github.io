As a Resident Assistant, I supported a residential floor of more than 45 students through peer mentorship, community leadership, conflict resolution, and educational, service, and social programming.

The role required me to balance resident needs with administrative work, crisis response, policy enforcement, and coordination with Residential Life staff. It was one of my earliest leadership roles and helped me develop practical skills in communication, judgment, accountability, and supporting people through difficult situations.
