Served as a Resident Assistant for a residential floor of more than 45 students, providing peer support, community leadership, conflict resolution, and programming focused on education, service, and social connection.

The role required balancing day-to-day resident support with administrative responsibilities, crisis response, policy enforcement, and coordination with Residential Life staff. It was an early leadership role that helped build practical experience in communication, judgment, accountability, and supporting people through a wide range of situations.

**Focus areas:** student support, community leadership, peer mentorship, conflict resolution, programming, crisis response, communication, accountability
