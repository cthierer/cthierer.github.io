This site uses [Umami](https://umami.is/), a privacy-focused, cookie-free analytics service, to measure aggregate traffic and interactions such as résumé downloads and outbound link clicks.

The analytics collected may include pages visited, referral source, approximate location, browser, device type, and interactions with selected links. This site does not use advertising trackers, create user profiles, or attempt to identify individual visitors.

No personal information is intentionally collected through analytics.
