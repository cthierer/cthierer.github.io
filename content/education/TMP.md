I attended UCLA Extension’s [Technical Management Program (<abbr>TMP</abbr>)](https://www.uclaextension.edu/engineering/technical-management-program), a 5-day focused leadership program for managers in engineering, IT, and other technical fields.

The program emphasized the people side of technical management, including communication, decision-making, and leading change. I continue to use several of its practices in my work: asking [innovative questions](https://podcasts.apple.com/cy/podcast/tell-less-ask-more/id1512818420?i=1000491280418) when helping engineers work through problems, identifying the biggest source of resistance when introducing change, and adapting my communication style to the person and situation.
